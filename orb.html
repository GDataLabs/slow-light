The full details…

The orb is an automated guide, not a clinician. It can misunderstand you and say things that are wrong, and it is built only for gentle relaxation — never a substitute for professional help. If things feel heavy more days than not, bring that to a real person; in the US you can also text HOME to 741741, and in immediate danger call your local emergency number.

Voice is optional. After each question the orb listens for a few seconds — a small waveform shows exactly when it can hear you, and typing or sliding always works instead. When ElevenLabs is set up (⚙, top right), your short voice answers are sent there to be turned into text, and the orb's own lines and fixed scene descriptions are sent there to be spoken and drawn; otherwise your browser's own speech service is used. This page keeps nothing on any server. Motion here is slow, there is no strobing, and your device's reduce-motion setting is respected.

The orb's voice

The built-in browser voice works everywhere but varies by device. If you have an ElevenLabs account, the orb can borrow one of its voices instead.

No key is set up on this device. Paste one here — it is kept only in this browser, and sent nowhere except directly to ElevenLabs.

Generated scenery

A dreamlike generated image (fast, cheap) or a slowly moving video (slower to arrive, costs more credits) appears softly behind the particles. The scene works fine while it's on its way — or without it.

What reaches ElevenLabs: the orb's own lines, fixed scene descriptions for sound and scenery, and — when you answer out loud — that short voice clip, to be turned into text. What you type never goes anywhere. Everything generated is cached on this device so repeats don't spend credits, and if ElevenLabs can't be reached the orb falls back to the browser voice and plain scenery rather than stopping.
