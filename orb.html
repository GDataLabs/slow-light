Slow Light

The Orb

A quiet guide in a dark, slow space. It will ask where you are and where you'd like to be, and then shape a place around your answers — somewhere to breathe, let a few things drift off, and land somewhere softer. You can answer it out loud, by typing, or with a slider. Nothing you say or type leaves this device.

Before you begin: the orb is an automated guide, not a person and not a clinician. It can misunderstand you and it can say things that are wrong. It is built only for gentle relaxation — it is not therapy, diagnosis, or crisis support, and it is never a substitute for professional help. If things feel heavy more days than not, please bring that to a real person. In the US, call or text 988 (24/7) or text HOME to 741741; elsewhere findahelpline.com lists local lines. In immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

Voice is optional; the mic is used only if you tap it, only while it pulses, and audio is processed by your browser's own speech service. If you switch the orb's voice to ElevenLabs in settings (⚙, top right), only the orb's own lines are sent there to be spoken — nothing you say or type ever is. Motion here is slow and there is no strobing. Your device's reduce-motion setting is respected.

The orb's voice

The built-in browser voice works everywhere but varies by device. If you have an ElevenLabs account, the orb can borrow one of its voices instead.

The key stays in this browser only — it is never shown to anyone or sent anywhere except directly to ElevenLabs. Only the orb's own lines are sent there to be spoken; nothing you say or type ever is. Spoken lines are cached on this device so repeat lines don't spend credits. If ElevenLabs can't be reached, the orb falls back to the browser voice mid-session rather than going silent.
