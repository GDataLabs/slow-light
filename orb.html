The Orb

A quiet guide in a dark, slow space. It will ask where you are and where you'd like to be, and then shape a place around your answers — somewhere to breathe, let a few things drift off, and land somewhere softer. You can answer it out loud, by typing, or with a slider. Nothing you say or type leaves this device.

Before you begin: the orb is an automated guide, not a person and not a clinician. It can misunderstand you and it can say things that are wrong. It is built only for gentle relaxation — it is not therapy, diagnosis, or crisis support, and it is never a substitute for professional help. If things feel heavy more days than not, please bring that to a real person. In the US, call or text 988 (24/7) or text HOME to 741741; elsewhere findahelpline.com lists local lines. In immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
