Slow Light is a self-guided relaxation tool, not medical treatment, and it does not diagnose or monitor anything. Any pulse figure it shows is a rough visual estimate, not a measurement. If you're in crisis, please reach a person — a friend, a clinician, or a local crisis line. In the US, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 24/7) or text HOME to 741741; outside the US, findahelpline.com lists local lines. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number. Motion is deliberately slow and there is no strobing, but if you're photosensitive or prone to motion sickness, use the reduce-motion setting on your device; this page follows it.
